Remove the background from a product photo — on your own device

Drop a photo, get a transparent PNG or a white-background JPG. A BiRefNet segmentation model runs entirely in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded and there is no account to create.

How it works

  1. Choose a photo

    JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 20 MB and 4096 px. The file is read locally and is never sent anywhere.

  2. The model runs in your browser

    The first visit downloads the model once (~58 MB) and caches it. Inference then runs on your device — on the GPU when a real one is present, otherwise on the CPU.

  3. Download the cut-out

    Get a transparent PNG (default), a white-background JPG, or a WebP. Adjust the mask edge first if you need to.

    What it does well, and where it struggles
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Measured on 64 images
    
            
    Every image decoded, ran, and exported at its original size, with no crash. Inference averages about 4 seconds per image on the CPU path.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Runs on the CPU by default
    
            
    No GPU is required. A modern hardware GPU is used automatically when one is present, but a software or older GPU is deliberately skipped because it measured slower or less accurate than the CPU.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Honest limits
    
            
    Thin structures (spokes, jewellery) and very low-contrast backgrounds are the hardest. On our 64-image set, 8 outputs came out empty — several of them dark or low-contrast frames.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Private by construction
    
            
    There is no upload code path. A restrictive Content-Security-Policy blocks third-party scripts, and the model is pinned by SHA-256 and cached on your device.
    
          
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Questions
    
        
    
          Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
          
    No. The image is read in your browser and processed by a model running on your device. No image pixels are sent to a server, and there is no account or sign-in.
    
        
    
        
    
          Why does it download something the first time?
          
    The AI model is about 58 MB. It downloads once and is then cached on your device, so later visits work offline and start immediately.
    
        
    
        
    
          Do I need a fast graphics card?
          
    No. It runs on the CPU with WebAssembly, which works on essentially any modern browser. If you have a recent hardware GPU it is used automatically for speed; older or software GPUs are skipped because they measured slower or wrong.
    
        
    
        
    
          What formats and sizes can I use?
          
    JPEG, PNG, or WebP input up to 20 MB and 4096 pixels on the longest side. Output is a transparent PNG (default), a white-background JPG, or a WebP.
    
        
    
        
    
          Is the result always perfect?
          
    No. Clean product silhouettes come out well. Transparent objects, very thin structures, and low-contrast backgrounds are harder — the edge can be soft or the subject can be missed. Use the mask controls in Advanced options, or try a photo with more separation between subject and background.
