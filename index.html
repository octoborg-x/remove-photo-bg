How SmartCutAI Works In Your Browser

A frictionless pipeline engineered for high performance, device autonomy, and uncompromising privacy.

01

Drop Photo

Accepts PNG, JPG, or WebP up to 20 MB and 4096 px. Decoded directly into local canvas memory without sending a single byte over the network.

02

Neural Segmentation

BiRefNet Lite runs via onnxruntime-web. The model executes with WebGPU when hardware acceleration is present, falling back to multi-threaded SIMD WASM.

Probability tensors upscale back to your image's full native resolution. Threshold cut-off and feather radii are tunable in real time.

Plain-Language Reference

What SmartCutAI Supports

No jargon required. Here is exactly what works, what it runs on, and what you get back.

Photos you can drop in

PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 20 MB and 4096 pixels on the longest side. Files are read as-is — the app never trusts the file name, and a mislabelled or corrupt image is caught and explained instead of silently failing.

Images it handles well

Product shots, portraits, clothing, accessories, hair and fur, glass and transparent items, and busy or flat backgrounds. Very wide or very tall images (like banners and logos) are padded with a faithful copy of their edges, so the subject is not stretched out of shape. When the AI keeps the whole frame, the app falls back to a classic edge-based cut-out so you still get a clean result.

Where it runs

Any modern browser. It uses the GPU automatically when a real, capable graphics chip is available (WebGPU), and otherwise runs on the CPU with multi-threaded WebAssembly. Software-only "fake GPUs" and known-slow mobile chips are detected and steered to the CPU, which is faster and more accurate there. You can override the choice in the Studio's advanced options.

What you get back

A transparent PNG by default, a studio-white JPG for marketplaces, or a compact WebP that keeps transparency. You can also place the cut-out on a solid colour, on pure white, or leave it transparent — and tune the mask's edge with a threshold and a feather slider before you download.

Speed and the one-time download

The model is about 58 MB and downloads once, then stays cached on your device. Inference is typically a few seconds per photo on the CPU and faster on a GPU. On a metered mobile link the app asks before that first download. Large photos are composited in bounded strips off the main thread, so the page stays responsive.

Privacy and what is never done

Your photo never leaves your device — there is no upload path in the app at all. No account, no watermark, no per-image limit, and no third-party scripts or fonts loaded. Everything runs in your browser, and you can verify it in your browser's Network tab or by going fully offline.

Curious how the cut-out is assembled? The compositing and preview layer is adapted from rembg-webgpu — Background Removal Library provided by www.rembg.com.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded to any server or cloud database? ▾

No, never. SmartCutAI is completely client-side. The entire neural network executes in your browser's WebAssembly sandbox. You can verify this by checking your browser's Network tab or even running the tool completely offline.

Why is there a one-time download when opening the app? ▾

The AI model weights (~58 MB) download once on your first visit. They are automatically cached in your browser's persistent Cache API, meaning subsequent visits start instantly without downloading anything again.

Do I need a discrete graphics card (GPU)? ▾

No. SmartCutAI is optimized to run smoothly on standard CPUs using multi-threaded WebAssembly SIMD. If your computer or phone has a supported modern GPU with WebGPU, it automatically accelerates inference for even faster processing.

What image file formats and resolutions are supported? ▾

You can upload PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 20 MB and 4096 px on the longest side. Exports are available in transparent PNG, studio-white JPG, or compressed WebP.
