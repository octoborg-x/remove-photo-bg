04 · Plain-Language Reference

What SmartCutAI Supports

Exact formats, hardware rules, and privacy guarantees — without vague marketing claims.

Photos you can drop in

PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 20 MB and 4096 pixels on the longest side. Files are read as-is — mislabelled or corrupt files are caught and explained cleanly.

Images it handles well

Product shots, portraits, clothing, accessories, hair and fur, glass and transparent items, and busy backgrounds. Extreme banners are padded safely and flooded masks invoke border-flood recovery.

Where it runs

Runs in any modern browser. Uses WebGPU automatically on verified hardware GPUs and falls back to multi-threaded SIMD WebAssembly on CPUs and mobile chips, with manual override in Engine Controls.

What you get back

Transparent PNG by default, marketplace JPG on pure white or any chosen colour, or compact WebP with alpha — plus live threshold, feather, and brush retouch.

Speed and the one-time download

The quantized model is ~58 MB, downloads once into your browser Cache API, and stays offline-ready. Metered mobile connections ask before downloading.

Privacy and zero upload path

Your photos never leave device memory — there is zero image upload code in the app, zero third-party font CDNs, and strict COOP/COEP isolation headers.

05 · Documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded to any server or cloud database? ▾

No, never. SmartCutAI is completely client-side. The neural network runs inside your browser's WebAssembly or WebGPU sandbox. You can verify this in the Network tab or run the tool completely offline once cached.

Why is there a one-time download when opening the app? ▾

The quantized model weights (~58 MB) download once on your first visit and persist in your browser's Cache API, so later visits launch instantly with zero network download.

Do I need a discrete graphics card (GPU)? ▾

No. SmartCutAI runs reliably on standard CPUs via multi-threaded WebAssembly SIMD, and automatically uses hardware WebGPU acceleration when available on your desktop GPU.

What image file formats and resolutions are supported? ▾

Inputs support PNG, JPEG, and WebP up to 20 MB and 4096 px on the longest side (mobile devices scale oversized photos down safely in local memory). Exports include transparent PNG, studio-white JPG, and compressed WebP.

Why it helps. The model always works at 512 × 512, so a big photo produces a mask that must be stretched to full size. Running 2 × 2 overlapping tiles makes each mask stretch about half as far, so the edges of a subject the model already found come out crisper — finer hair, cleaner glass rims.

What it cannot do. Tiling only sharpens an edge; it does not find a subject the model missed.

Cost. Each tile is a full 512px run, so 2 × 2 is about four times slower and 3 × 3 about nine times.

Stitching. Neighbouring tiles overlap by 25% and are blended with a linear crossfade whose weights sum to one.

Tip. Below ~1024px on the long side the model already sees the photo at about 1×.
